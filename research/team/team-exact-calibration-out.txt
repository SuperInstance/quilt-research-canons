  CHECK ON THE BRIEF'S lambda_2 ~ 4*pi^2/n^2: CONFIRMED, not assumed. The smallest
  nonzero eigenvalue of a Kronecker sum is the smallest nonzero eigenvalue of ONE
  factor, not the sum of both -- so lambda_2 = min(2-2cos(2pi/a), 2-2cos(2pi/b))
  = 4*sin^2(pi/max(a,b)) + O(max^-4) ~ 4*pi^2/max(a,b)^2. For the square torus that
  is the brief's 4*pi^2/n^2 with n = side length, and the relative error column above
  is the O(n^-2) that the asymptotic is entitled to. (An earlier draft of this script
  summed the two factors instead of minimising, which doubles lambda_2; the exact
  eigenvalue here is what settles it.)
