  A claim with no receipt is not a wrong claim. It is an UNGROUNDED one, and the

  THE HEADLINE CASE IS THE INTERESTING ONE. lane-u-headline cites a receipt that

  the artifact it cites says the opposite. Timestamps catch ORDER, not SENSE.

  SO THE FLEET NEEDS TWO GATES, AND THEY CATCH DIFFERENT THINGS:
    GATE 1  grounding lint (this file)  -- catches claims made before evidence
    GATE 2  internal-consistency read -- catches a headline its own body refutes
  Neither substitutes for the other. A receipt that predates a claim does not
  mean the claim agrees with the receipt.

    synthetic claim with NO receipt      -> UNSOURCED   PASS
